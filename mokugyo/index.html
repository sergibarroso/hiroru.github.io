Mokugyo

Zen timer & Heart Sutra chant for iPhone and Apple Watch

Coming soon to the App Store.

Mokugyo is a meditation app rooted in Zen practice and open to any style. It has two parts: a timer for silent sitting, and the Heart Sutra, chanted in time with a mokugyo — the wooden fish that keeps the beat in Zen temples.

Sit in silence

Tap Begin and a singing bowl opens your sitting. A short countdown helps you settle, then your chosen time passes in silence: 5 to 60 minutes on iPhone, 5 to 45 on Apple Watch. When it ends, the bowl sounds again and rings out fully. The bowl was measured from a recording of a real singing bowl and is created as it sounds — there are no audio files.

Chant the Heart Sutra

Tap the heart to chant the Hannya Shingyō (般若心経), the Heart Sutra as chanted in Japanese Zen temples. Each character is shown in kanji with its reading beneath, and lights up in turn as it is chanted. A twelve-second countdown gives you time to sing the opening line, maka hannya haramita shingyō, as the temple bell sounds and fades; then the mokugyo strikes on every character, guiding your voice to the closing mantra. Choose the pace and the text size that suit you in Preferences.

On your wrist, and in Health

The Apple Watch app does both, and keeps going with your wrist down. With your permission, each sitting is saved to the Health app as Mindful Minutes.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing you do in Mokugyo leaves your device.
